
explosion_hotds.tscn was created based on this Brackeys tutorial https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=htRjt505sPg and was inspired by House of the Dying Sun. It's used on small fighters as their final death explosion and does change environment brightness if you're looking right at it.

frigate_explosion.tscn is also based on the above Brackeys tutorial. This explosion is used for all mid-sized capital ships.

vfx_explosion_lelu_single.tscn was created based on this tutorial https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miQ7DO1QUVE It uses a shader. It's used on the flak bullet area explosions.

vfx_explosion_lelu_8x.tscn was created based on this tutorial https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miQ7DO1QUVE It uses a shader. It's used on small fighters as their initial kill explosion.

vfx_explosion_lelu_tutorial was created based on this tutorial https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miQ7DO1QUVE It uses a shader. Turrets use it on death.
